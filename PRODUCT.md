# Nyx Portfolio

## Product Purpose

This is Arne Decant's personal portfolio for communicating senior software engineering, frontend architecture, product ownership, design-system work, and creative technology experience. It should help technically sophisticated visitors understand the kind of complex product problems Arne can own and make credible contact or opportunity conversations easy to start.

## Users

- Engineering leaders and product teams evaluating senior engineering experience
- Collaborators interested in frontend architecture, design systems, and interactive systems
- Technical peers exploring the public projects and experiments

## Register

brand

## Tone

Confident, mature, specific, personal, and technically grounded. The visual language is nocturnal technical and atmospheric minimalism: generous negative space, editorial typography, precise structural lines, and restrained cyan, violet, and amber signals.

## Principles

- Present truthful, source-backed work without invented metrics or confidential details.
- Make complex systems feel understandable without flattening their important constraints.
- Prefer reusable Nyx Kit primitives for standard interaction behavior and bespoke components for portfolio-specific composition.
- Keep the static GitHub Pages deployment path viable and preserve responsive, accessible behavior.

## Anti-References

Avoid generic developer templates, corporate SaaS landing pages, resume dashboards, terminal portfolios, neon cyberpunk, arbitrary logo walls, fake code decoration, stock photography, and animation that blocks reading or navigation.
