* Off-white typography
* Muted blue, violet or cool cyan as the primary accent

* Fine lines, soft glows, translucent layers and subtle grain
* Large editorial typography combined with a clean technical sans-serif
* Strong spacing and asymmetric but well-balanced compositions
* Rounded corners only where appropriate; avoid excessive floating rounded cards
* Clear visual hierarchy and excellent readability

* Generic gradient blobs
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Skill progress bars or percentage ratings
* A wall of technology logos

* Stock photography
* Unnecessary 3D objects such as floating laptops
